How TrailBlazing reads IGN data

Paths come live from IGN's BD TOPO (road section layer, troncon_de_route) through the Géoplateforme. Zoom in to level 14 or more to load them.

Green: section whose IGN nature is Chemin, Sentier, Route empierrée or Escalier, with no "private" flag and no "reserved to authorised users" access. These are the best candidates for chemins ruraux (old chemins vicinaux), which are open to public use (Code rural, art. L161-1 and following).

Red dotted: IGN marks the section as private or reserved to authorised users (Restreint aux ayants droit).

Limits. IGN does not record legal ownership, so a green track can still be a private chemin d'exploitation, and a mayor can restrict motor vehicles by local order. Turn on the Cadastre overlay: a path running between parcels is usually public, one crossing inside a numbered parcel is usually private. Always respect signs on the ground.
